# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

GreyAuction operates an online auction platform for vehicles, watches, art, electronics, jewellery and property, together with the bidding, invoicing, payment, escrow and payout services that go with it. Running an auction means handling personal data about buyers, sellers and the people who work for them. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

It is written to meet the information requirements of the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023** (the "NDPA"), which applies to us because we are established in Nigeria and process personal data in the course of running the platform. Our separate **Data Protection** statement sets out how we comply with the Act in operational detail — the lawful basis for each activity, how each of your rights is handled, and our security, breach and transfer arrangements.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

GreyAuction is the **data controller** for the personal data described here: we decide what is collected and why. Where we use another company to process data on our behalf — a payment provider, an email service — that company is a **data processor** acting on our written instructions, and remains accountable to us for what it does.

- **Controller:** GreyAuction Ltd, Lagos, Nigeria
- **Data Protection Officer:** privacy@greyauction.com
- **Data protection queries and rights requests:** privacy@greyauction.com, or open a ticket on the Contact page and mark it "Data protection"

We will respond to a rights request within the period the NDPA allows and will tell you if we need more information to identify you.

## 3. Our lawful basis

The NDPA requires a lawful basis for every purpose. Ours are:

- **Contract** — most of what we do is performance of our agreement with you: registering you, running the auction you bid in, invoicing, taking payment, holding funds in escrow, paying sellers, delivering lots and answering support requests.
- **Legal obligation** — keeping accounting and tax records, verifying seller identity, responding to a lawful request from a regulator or court.
- **Legitimate interests** — keeping the platform secure (fraud detection, rate limiting, audit logging), enforcing our Terms, and improving the service. We balance these against your rights and you may object to them (§6).
- **Consent** — marketing email, and any optional feature that asks for it. Consent is separate from the contract: withdrawing it never stops you bidding or selling.

Where consent is the basis, you can withdraw it at any time; that does not affect processing already carried out. Marketing subscriptions use a double opt-in, and every message includes a working unsubscribe.

## 4. Sensitive personal data

Identity documents you upload for verification are treated as sensitive personal data under the NDPA. They are:

- collected only when verification is required — a buyer never has to upload identity documents to bid;
- stored separately from public content, in private object storage that is not publicly addressable;
- visible only to you and to the reviewer handling your verification, and returned to you through short-lived links rather than public URLs;
- deleted or anonymised when the verification record no longer needs to be kept (§5).

We do not process data revealing health, religion, ethnicity, political opinions or trade-union membership, and we ask you not to include such information in free-text fields such as dispute descriptions or messages.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it, or as long as the law requires:

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account and profile | While your account is open, then deactivated. Profile data is removed when there is no legal or accounting reason to keep it. |
| Identity verification documents | For the period required to demonstrate we verified you, then deleted. A record that verification took place is retained without the document images. |
| Invoices, payment records, wallet ledger and payout records | Kept as accounting records for the period Nigerian tax and company law requires; they are not deleted on account closure. |
| Bids, sale records, escrow history | Kept with the sale record so a dispute or audit can be answered. |
| Support tickets, disputes, messages | Kept for a limited period after the matter closes, then deleted. |
| Reviews | Published reviews are retained while the seller profile is live, since removing them would mislead other bidders. |
| Audit and security logs | Kept for a short operational window, then deleted. |
| Marketing subscriptions | Until you unsubscribe, plus a suppression record so you are not re-added. |

When a retention period ends we delete the data or irreversibly anonymise it. Because sale and payout records must be retained, closing your account removes your profile and stops all processing for marketing or contact, but does not erase the financial record of a completed sale.

## 6. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may ask us to:

- **Confirm and access** — tell you whether we hold your data and give you a copy.
- **Correct** — fix inaccurate or incomplete data. Most of this you can do yourself in Account settings; for anything else, ask us.
- **Delete** — erase data we no longer have a lawful reason to keep.
- **Restrict** — pause processing while a dispute about accuracy or lawfulness is resolved.
- **Object** — stop processing based on our legitimate interests, including any profiling.
- **Portability** — receive the data you gave us in a structured, commonly used format.
- **Withdraw consent** — at any time, for anything based on consent.
- **Not be subject to a decision made solely by automated means** where it has a legal or similarly significant effect on you (see §9).
- **Complain** — to us first, and to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if we do not resolve it.

To exercise any of these, email privacy@greyauction.com from the address on your account, or open a Contact ticket. We may ask for proof of identity where the request concerns sensitive records. There is no charge unless a request is manifestly unfounded or repetitive.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies and local storage entries, all of which are needed to run the platform:

- **Session cookie** — proves who you are after sign-in. It is HTTP-only and encrypted, so page scripts cannot read it. Without it you cannot stay signed in.
- **Preference storage** — remembers your language, consent choices and a few display settings.
- **Offline cache** — if you install the app to your home screen, a service worker caches pages and assets so it opens quickly. It holds no personal data beyond what a normal page load would.

We do not run third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Traffic reaches us through Cloudflare, which processes technical request data as our processor for security and delivery. You can clear cookies in your browser at any time; clearing the session cookie signs you out.

## 8. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where the service requires it, and only the minimum necessary:

- **Payment providers** — to take a payment or make a payout. They receive the amount, the reference and the identity details their own regulation requires.
- **Identity verification reviewers** — where a seller's documents must be checked.
- **Email and SMS providers** — to send invoices, verification codes, invitations, outbid notices and, if you opted in, announcements. They receive the address and the message, not your account history.
- **Cloud hosting, storage and security providers** — to serve the site and store files, including your uploaded documents.
- **Sign-in providers** — if you choose to sign in with Google, Google tells us your verified email address and name.
- **Sellers and buyers in a transaction** — the counterparty to a sale sees what completing it requires: the name and contact details needed for collection or delivery, and the delivery address for a shipped lot. They never see your payment details, wallet balance or bidding limits.
- **Professional advisers, auditors, regulators and courts** — where the law requires it or where it is necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.
- **A successor entity** — if the business is sold or merged, data may transfer to the buyer under the same terms.

**We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.**

Where a supplier processes data outside Nigeria, we rely on the transfer provisions of the NDPA and tell you about it — see the Data Protection statement for the country-by-country position and the safeguards in place.

## 9. Automated decisions and profiling

Two parts of the platform reach conclusions about you without a human in the loop for the first step:

- **Listing screening.** Every lot is screened automatically for prohibited goods, counterfeits, misleading claims and stolen or untraceable items. A high score can hold a listing for review. It never publishes a lot on its own, and an administrator makes every publish decision — you can ask for that decision to be reviewed by a person.
- **Seller risk scoring.** A compliance score and risk band are calculated from your verification status, document completeness and account standing. It is **advisory**: it informs the administrator who decides whether you may publish, and it does not by itself block you.

Neither of these makes a final decision with legal effect about you. Where you believe an automated outcome is wrong, contact us and a person will review it. We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions about creditworthiness.

## 10. Keeping it secure

We protect personal data with measures proportionate to what it is:

- passwords and wallet PINs are stored only as one-way hashes, never in readable form;
- sign-in sessions are held in encrypted, HTTP-only cookies that page scripts cannot read;
- privileged actions are role-restricted and written to an audit trail recording who did what, when, and from which address;
- repeat-sensitive actions (sign-in, code entry, PIN attempts, bidding) are rate-limited;
- identity documents sit in private storage, not on the public internet;
- access to production data is limited to staff who need it for their role.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and affected users as the NDPA requires — see the Data Protection statement for the timelines.

## 11. Children

The platform is for adults. You must be at least 18 to register, bid or sell; we do not knowingly collect data from children, and a bid is a binding contract that a child cannot enter into. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will close it and delete the data we are not required to keep. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when what we do changes — a new supplier, a new purpose, a new retention period. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version, and material changes will be announced on the platform or by email before they take effect. Continuing to use GreyAuction after a change means you accept the updated policy.

## 13. Contact and complaints

Start with us: **privacy@greyauction.com**, or a Contact ticket marked "Data protection". We will investigate and reply.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission**, the regulator established by the NDPA. We will cooperate with any investigation and will not penalise you for exercising your rights.
