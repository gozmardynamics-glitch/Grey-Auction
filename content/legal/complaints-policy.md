# Complaints Policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

## 1. Our commitment

We would rather hear about a problem than not hear about it. If something has gone wrong — a bid you believe was mishandled, an invoice that does not add up, a lot that does not match its description, a payout that has not arrived, or the way someone on our team dealt with you — tell us and we will investigate it properly.

This policy explains what counts as a complaint, how to raise one, what we will do and by when, how to escalate it if our answer is not good enough, and where else you can go. **Lodging a complaint costs you nothing**, and raising one will never affect your ability to bid, sell or use the platform.

It applies to any expression of dissatisfaction about our products, services or the conduct of our staff or users, where you expect a response or a resolution — whether or not you call it a complaint.

## 2. How we see our role

GreyAuction is an online auction platform operating in Nigeria. **We act as agent between sellers and buyers**: the seller owns the lot and is responsible for its description and condition; we run the auction, invoice the buyer, hold the money in escrow until the sale is complete, and pay the seller. Understanding that split will tell you quickly whether your complaint is about us or about a seller — and we will tell you plainly which it is when we respond.

We commit to dealing with every complaint **fairly, consistently and impartially**. A complaint against a seller is investigated by someone who is not that seller's account manager, and a complaint about a decision we made is reviewed by someone who did not make it. Your information is handled confidentially, in line with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) and [Data Protection](/data-protection) statement.

## 3. How to raise a complaint

Use the channel that matches your problem — it is the difference between a fast answer and a slow one:

| If your complaint is about… | Raise it here | Why this channel |
|---|---|---|
| **A specific purchase**: item not as described, never delivered, money still held, wrong amount charged | **Open a dispute from the purchase** (your dashboard → purchase → dispute, or the escrow panel) | A dispute freezes the escrow funds while we investigate, so the money cannot move before the matter is decided |
| **A bid, an auction, a reserve, a lot that closed unexpectedly** | Contact form, topic "Bidding and auctions" | It is about the auction itself, not a transaction, so no funds need freezing |
| **A payment that failed, an invoice query, a duplicate charge** | Contact form, topic "Payments and invoices" | We need the invoice number and payment reference to trace it |
| **A payout or bank detail change** | Contact form, topic "Seller payouts", or your seller payment screen | Payout matters are handled by the team that can see the wallet ledger |
| **Delivery or collection** | Contact form, topic "Delivery and collection" | The shipment record is the evidence we work from |
| **Sign-in, verification, suspension, account access** | Contact form, topic "Account, verification and access" | Verification outcomes are re-reviewed by the reviewer's supervisor |
| **A review you believe is false or abusive** | Flag the review on the seller's profile | It records the flag against the exact review |
| **How we handled your personal data** | Data protection: **privacy@greyauction.com** (our Data Protection Officer) | Data protection complaints follow the statutory route, described in the Data Protection statement |
| **Anything else** | Contact form, topic "Something else" | We will route it internally |

You can also write to us at **GreyAuction Ltd, Lagos, Nigeria**, or email **support@greyauction.com**. Please do not send identity documents by email — upload them through the verification panel instead.

**If you need help making a complaint** — because of a disability, language, or difficulty using the platform — tell us and we will take the complaint in another way. The platform is available in English, French and Dutch, and we will communicate in whatever form works for you. If you appoint someone to deal with us on your behalf, we may ask for written authority before discussing your account with them.

## 4. What we will do, and when

| Stage | What happens | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| **Acknowledgement** | We confirm we have received your complaint and give you a **reference number**. Every complaint gets one. | Within **2 business days** |
| **First response** | A person reads it properly and tells you what we understand the problem to be, what we need from you, and what we think will happen next | Within **2 business days** of acknowledgement |
| **Investigation** | We look at the record — the bid log, the invoice, the escrow hold, the messages, the shipment — and where the complaint is about a seller's conduct, we hear from both sides | Timeframe given to you at first response |
| **Resolution** | We tell you the outcome and **why**, what we are doing about it, and what to do if you disagree | Within **10 business days** for most complaints; **20 business days** where a third party must be involved (a payment provider, a carrier, a seller who has to respond) |
| **Updates** | If we cannot hit a date, we tell you before it passes rather than after — with a new date and the reason | Proactively |

Where we can resolve it at the first point of contact, we will. The person handling your complaint will give you their first name, and will be the person who stays with it.

**Disputes move differently**, because money is involved: opening a dispute immediately flags the escrow hold, and the funds stay where they are until the dispute is resolved. We may ask both sides for evidence, including photographs or the uploaded condition report. The outcome is either release of the funds to the seller or a refund to you. A refunded dispute also unwinds the sale.

## 5. What to include

The faster you give us these, the faster we can decide:

- your **invoice number**, lot number or room name — or the dispute reference if you already opened one;
- **what you expected** and **what happened**, with dates;
- any **evidence** — photographs, the condition report, messages, a payment confirmation or bank statement;
- the **outcome you want** — an apology, a correction, a partial refund, a full refund, a rescheduled delivery. You are not bound by what you ask for, and asking for a specific remedy helps us assess your complaint against it.

## 6. If you are not satisfied with our response

**Internal review.** If you believe we got it wrong, reply to the same reference and ask for a **review**. It will be looked at by someone senior who was not involved the first time — for a payment or escrow matter, by the finance lead; for conduct, by the head of operations. We will confirm the review within 2 business days and aim to complete it within 10.

**External review.** If you remain unsatisfied, you are free to take the matter further, free of charge:

- **Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC)** — consumer complaints about goods, services and unfair commercial conduct;
- **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)** — if your complaint concerns how we handled your personal data;
- **The courts of Nigeria**, or a recognised alternative dispute resolution centre such as the Lagos Multi-Door Courthouse, if the matter is a legal claim;
- **Your bank or card issuer**, for a card payment you believe was wrongly taken.

We will cooperate fully with any of them.

## 7. What we ask of you

We will treat you with respect, and we ask the same in return. Please do not abuse or threaten our staff: behaviour that puts anyone's safety at risk will be recorded, may be reported to the police, and may lead to your account being restricted.

Complaints that are **knowingly false, repetitive after a final decision, or made to harass** will be closed, and we will tell you why. A complaint we close for that reason does not stop you from raising a **different, genuine** issue later.

## 8. What we do with complaints

Every complaint is logged with its outcome, and we review them as a group — not just one at a time. We look for patterns: a lot category that repeatedly draws "not as described", a payment method that keeps failing, a step in collection that confuses people. Where a pattern shows a defect in the platform, we fix the platform rather than answering the same complaint next month. Complaints are also input to the seller reviews and account decisions that keep the marketplace honest.

We publish nothing about individual complaints without your consent. Aggregate trends may be reported internally and, in summary, to our board.

## 9. Your rights

Nothing in this policy reduces your rights under our [Terms & Conditions](/terms), the [Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023](/data-protection), the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act, or any other law that applies. You can always take independent action; we simply ask that you raise the matter with us first, because most complaints are resolved faster that way.
