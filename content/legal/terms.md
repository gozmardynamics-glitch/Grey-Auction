## 1. Who we are, and what we are not

We operate an online auction platform. **We act as agent for the seller**: the seller owns the lot and is responsible for it; we run the auction, invoice you, hold your payment in escrow and pay the seller out of it. An invoice from us is a demand for the amount shown, not a warranty that we own the lot.

We are not a party to the sale contract between buyer and seller, and we do not take title to any lot. Our role is to run a fair process, hold the money safely and enforce these terms on both sides.

Identity verification: buyers need a verified email address. **Sellers must complete verification and be granted publishing consent before a lot can be published** — being verified is not the same as being allowed to publish.

## 3. Bidding is binding

**A bid is an offer to buy at that price, and cannot be withdrawn.** Before you confirm, check the amount, the minimum increment and the fee breakdown shown on the lot page. The platform also imposes a short cooldown between bids on the same lot to prevent accidental double submits.

Some lots carry a **reserve price**: if bidding does not reach it, the lot does not sell. Some lots are offered **subject to the seller's approval** of the final bid; if the seller does not respond within the published window, the lot closes without a sale and neither party owes the other anything.

Auto-bidding (proxy bidding): you set a maximum, and the system bids the minimum needed to keep you in front, never above your maximum. A standing auto-bid takes priority over a one-off bid of the same amount.

## 4. Fees and the total you pay

The hammer price is not the total. On top of it, the **buyer's premium** and **VAT** apply, and occasionally fixed or other charges. The lot page shows the breakdown before you bid, and the invoice repeats it. The invoice is the record of what you were charged.

As a seller you pay a **seller commission** and any applicable charges, deducted before your proceeds reach your wallet. The percentages are set by the platform and shown in your fee settings; you may switch individual fees on or off where your agreement allows, but you do not set the rates.

Fees are reviewed from time to time. A change never applies to a bid you have already placed or a lot already approved.

## 5. Payment

Invoices are settled in **Nigerian naira (NGN)** and in a single payment — no instalments. The due date is on the invoice and is set by the seller's payment terms. We only offer payment methods we can confirm and settle; if a method is not shown at checkout, it is not available for that invoice.

Card details are entered on the payment provider's page and never reach our servers.

**If you do not pay:** an unpaid invoice remains due after its due date. We may cancel the sale, release the lot, restrict your account from bidding until the amount is settled, and charge any costs we have actually incurred. A pattern of non-payment is grounds for suspension (§11). If you believe an invoice is wrong, do not simply ignore it — raise a complaint before the due date and it will be looked at.

## 6. Escrow: how your money is held

When you pay, your money is held in escrow — not passed to the seller immediately, and not treated as our money. On release, the seller receives their net proceeds (the hammer price less the seller commission), the platform keeps its fees, and VAT is remitted by us and belongs to neither party.

The escrow window for each lot is shown on the lot page. It is your protection window: if what arrives does not match the lot, raise a **dispute** from the purchase before it closes and the funds stay where they are while we investigate. A dispute can be resolved by releasing the funds to the seller or refunding you; a partial refund can also be agreed, in which case the remainder still settles normally.

## 7. Collection, delivery and risk

Collection and delivery are the buyer's responsibility. Where delivery is offered, you enter an address at checkout and a shipment is created so you can follow it; the cost is quoted before you pay.

Risk in a lot passes to you when it is collected or delivered. Inspect promptly — a complaint about condition is much easier to resolve before the lot has moved on.

Lots are screened before publication for prohibited goods, counterfeits, misleading claims and stolen or untraceable items, and an administrator approves every lot; that screening is not a warranty to the buyer.

**Prohibited on this platform:** goods that cannot lawfully be sold in Nigeria, Counterfeit or replica items presented as genuine, items you do not own or are not authorised to sell, and any attempt to move a sale off-platform to avoid fees.

Once a lot is approved, its bidding terms — price, reserve, increment, auction type — are locked. Corrections need support, not a quiet edit under live bids.

Anyone may report a review for moderation. **A report does not remove it**: it goes into a moderation queue and an administrator decides whether to hide or remove it. A seller who disagrees with a review of their own business replies to it publicly, or raises a complaint if they believe it is false — a seller cannot report a review of themselves.
